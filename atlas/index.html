House rules

  1. Take turns naming a country, state or city.
  2. Your place must start with the last letter of the place before it. India → Australia.
  3. No place can be said twice.
  4. You have 30 seconds each turn.
  5. Everyone gets 3 hints for the whole game. In a turn, hint 1 tells the continent, hint 2 lights it up on the globe, hint 3 tells the second letter.
  6. Time runs out? You lose a hint.
  7. Your first mistake in a turn is a warning. After that, each mistake costs a hint.
  8. Out of hints and you lose another one? You're out. The last player left wins!

Rules made up by Arin.
